Check Out

Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Need a different time? Just ask. We can often accommodate unless another guest is checking in.

Your checklist (tap to tick)

  • Tell your host what time you are checking out
  • Reset the safe to its default code (see Check In)
  • Switch off and unplug the heaters
  • Take your garbage to the stairway bin
  • Check for your belongings, passport and chargers
  • Return the elevator key (see notes)

Pre-checkout inspection: someone will pass by to help with check-out and do a quick inspection.

Early or late flight? You may need to self check-out. Please tell us first and leave the elevator key with someone at the front desk.

Airport drop-off: need a ride? Let us know and we will help arrange it.

Feedback: we aim for 5 out of 5. Tell us about any issue, and how your tour guide did if we arranged one. It helps future guests.

Quick reference

House Rules & Terms

Wi-Fi

  • Wi-Fi is provided as a convenience. Your network details are in the Wi-Fi section once you unlock it.
  • Please use it for lawful purposes only.
  • No refunds for outages, speed or access issues, so download what you need in advance if you rely on it.

Building & Security

  • Keep the elevator key safe and do not lose it.
  • Make sure the apartment door is locked behind you every time you leave.
  • Only registered guests may stay overnight. Visitors are your responsibility.
  • Do not share your door code or the password to this page. Use the in-room safe for valuables.
  • Switch off and unplug heaters when you leave. Tell us if a smoke alarm chirps.

Trash Disposal

  • Leave garbage in the bin in the stairway, through the door in front of the middle elevator.
  • Collection is usually between 2 AM and 6 AM.
  • Please do not leave bags in the hallway or by the elevators.

Quiet Hours

  • Quiet hours run daily from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM.
  • Keep outdoor and balcony noise to a minimum, and be considerate of neighbors.
  • Repeated disturbances may end the stay without refund.

Checkout

  • Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Please tell us your plans.
  • Early or late flight? You may need to self check-out. Tell us first and leave the elevator key with the front desk.
  • Reset the safe, switch off heaters, take out the garbage and check for your belongings.

Damage & Terms

  • Guests are responsible for damage to or loss of the property and contents.
  • Damage, breakage and missing items are charged at full replacement value.
  • No smoking indoors (balconies and terraces only), and no parties or subletting.

Questions or problems during your stay? Contact your host. Thank you for taking care of our home.
