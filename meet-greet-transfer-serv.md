Meet and Greet (or Meet & Assist) services at Egypt airports are personalized assistance packages designed to make arrival smoother and faster, especially at busy airports like Cairo International (CAI). 

What it typically includes (Arrival) A professional greeter meets you right after you get off the plane (at the gate, airbridge, or sometimes with a buggy/electric cart) holding a sign with your name.  
They then help you with:

- Visa on arrival / immigration formalities (often using Fast Track / priority lanes)
- Passport control
- Collecting your luggage
- Going through customs
- Escorting you to the arrivals exit where your driver/hotel transfer is waiting
