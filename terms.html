Terms & Conditions

Reservation & check-in times

  • Check-in is from 3:00 PM. Check-out is by 11:00 AM.
  • Maximum capacity is 6 guests in total, adults and children combined.
  • Keys and digital access codes are issued only after we have received and verified ID for every occupant.

Guest identification (required by Egyptian regulations)

Official government ID is required for every occupant. Send digital copies before check-in and present them to building security on arrival.

  • Non-Arab foreigners: passport plus valid Egyptian entry visa or stamp for all adults.
  • Egyptian citizens: national ID card or Egyptian passport. Cohabitation proof is required: an official Egyptian marriage certificate.
  • Arab / GCC nationals: passport plus Egyptian entry visa or stamp. Cohabitation proof is required: an official home-country marriage certificate or family booklet.
  • Mixed parties (Egyptian and foreigner): the foreigner's passport and visa, plus the Egyptian's national ID or passport. Cohabitation proof is required: a notarized marriage certificate or a valid signed Orfi (customary) contract showing full legal identities.

Refusing or failing to provide the required documents is a guest default. Entry is refused and the reservation is cancelled with no refund.

House Rules

Occupancy & visitors

  • Overnight guests must never exceed the registered capacity (maximum 6). Unregistered guests are not permitted.
  • Daytime visitors are welcome between 8:00 AM and 10:00 PM only, pre-cleared with us and registered with building security.

Quiet hours & conduct

  • Quiet hours run daily from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM.
  • No parties, loud music or noisy gatherings. No subletting or commercial photography.

Non-smoking

  • No smoking indoors: cigarettes, cigars, shisha and e-cigarettes included.
  • Smoking is allowed only on open-air balconies and terraces.
  • Indoor smoking carries an automatic $200 USD odor-elimination fee.

Care of the apartment

  • You accept the property as clean and undamaged on arrival.
  • Damage, breakage, missing items or stains are charged at full current replacement value.
  • Excessive soiling needing specialist cleaning carries an extra fee of at least $100 USD (or the EGP equivalent).

Illegal activity

Illegal substances or weapons, or any act that breaches Egyptian civil or criminal law, lead to immediate eviction without refund and a report to the local Police or Tourism Police.

Legal Information

Property owner / lessor

Samer I. Hassaballa, owner and lessor of the Maadi Nile View apartment (Maadi Stars Towers, Tower 3A, Floor 12, Suite 5, Cairo, Egypt), managed by Sam's Spots Property Management.

Regulatory compliance

This tenancy is regulated under the Egyptian Civil Code and Ministry of Interior and national security directives for short-term rentals. The lessor may enter the property and end the agreement without judicial warning or prior notice where there is a failure to meet statutory requirements, unverified identity documents, or unauthorized conduct on the premises.

Liability

  • Assumption of risk: guests and occupants accept the risks of staying at the property, including personal injury or property damage.
  • Personal belongings: the lessor is not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged valuables, cash, jewelry or electronics.
  • Indemnification: the primary guest agrees to defend and hold harmless the lessor, building management and staff from claims arising from the guest's stay or negligence.
  • Utilities: the lessor is not liable for temporary outages beyond their control, such as power cuts, water shutdowns, internet outages or elevator repairs.

Emergency right of entry

The lessor or authorized building staff may enter immediately without prior consent for emergency maintenance threats (such as water leaks or gas smells), suspected illegal activity, noise complaints, or failure to provide required security documents.

Governing law & jurisdiction

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt. Any dispute falls under the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Cairo, Egypt.

Acceptance

By signing, paying for a reservation, or clicking "Agree & Continue", the primary guest confirms they have read and accepted all terms and house rules.
