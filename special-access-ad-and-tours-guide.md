## 2. Overview & Official Permit Pricing Structure

### What Are Special Access Tours?
Exclusive, permit-based experiences granted directly by Egypt’s Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities (and supervised by Tourism Police and official Antiquities Inspectors). These permits allow private entry into closed chambers, restricted archaeological sites, or world-famous monuments strictly outside normal public opening hours (early morning before opening or after-hours closing).

### Pricing & Permit Breakdown:
- **Base Ministry Special Access Permit Fee**: **$2,000 USD** *per site permit*[cite: 9] (Exclusive of private transfers, licensed Egyptologist guide fees, and luxury handling).
- **Variable Site Pricing**: Final total packages typically range between **$2,000 USD to $4,500+ USD** per private site, depending on group size, specific site permits requested, and custom luxury inclusions.
- **Booking Window**: Advance booking required (**minimum 14–30 days prior**) to clear government security and antiquities authorizations.

## 3. Special Access Sites Catalog Data

### Category 1: Giza Plateau Iconic Monuments
1. **Private Access Inside the Great Pyramid of Khufu (Cheops)**
   - **Permit Rate**: ~$2,000 USD permit baseline + tour handling ($2,500 - $3,500 total package).
   - **Access**: Full 1 to 2 hours inside *completely alone* after public hours[cite: 9]. Includes the King’s Chamber, Grand Gallery, plus restricted access to the **Queen’s Chamber** and **Subterranean Chamber ("The Pit")**[cite: 9].
   - **Image relative path**: `01_photos/02_tourism/special-access.jpg`

2. **Great Sphinx Enclosure "Between the Paws"**
   - **Permit Rate**: ~$2,000 USD permit baseline ($2,200 - $3,000 total package).
   - **Access**: Step inside the restricted enclosure wall right up to the feet of the Sphinx and the **Dream Stele of Thutmose IV**, away from the public viewing platform.

3. **Giza Plateau After-Hours & Osiris Shaft**
   - **Permit Rate**: ~$2,000 USD per permit area ($2,500 - $4,000 total package).
   - **Access**: Private access to the Pyramid of Menkaure, or deep subterranean descent into the multi-tiered **Osiris Shaft** near the Sphinx causeway.

---

### Category 2: Saqqara & Closed Archaeological Zones
1. **Step Pyramid of Djoser & Subterranean Labyrinth**
   - **Permit Rate**: ~$2,000 USD permit baseline ($2,300 - $3,000 total package).
   - **Access**: Exclusive private entry down into the blue-faience tile passages and deep granite burial shaft below Djoser's Step Pyramid.

2. **The Serapeum of Saqqara & Active Excavation Sites**
   - **Permit Rate**: ~$2,000 USD permit baseline ($2,200 - $2,800 total package).
   - **Access**: VIP entry to examine the massive 70-ton solid basalt/granite sarcophagi. Special permission add-ons available for permanently closed active dig sites at **Abu Sir (Abusir)** and **Abu Ghurab Sun Temples**.

---

### Category 3: Luxor & Upper Egypt VIP Access
1. **Valley of the Kings: Private Royal Tomb Closures**
   - **Permit Rate**: ~$2,000 - $3,500 USD (depending on tomb selection & hours).
   - **Access**: Private after-hours entry into restricted royal tombs (such as King Tutankhamun, Seti I, or Nefertari in Valley of the Queens) with zero public visitors present.

2. **Luxor Temple & Karnak Night Access**
   - **Permit Rate**: ~$2,000 USD permit baseline ($2,400 - $3,200 total package).
   - **Access**: Illuminate the great Hypostyle Hall or Luxor Temple colonnades after dark with exclusive private lighting and guide service.

---

### Category 4: Specialized Closed Antiquities
1. **The Osirion at Abydos (Temple of Seti I)**
   - **Permit Rate**: ~$2,000 - $2,500 USD permit baseline ($2,800 - $3,800 total package).
   - **Access**: Descend into the subterranean water-surrounded megalithic sanctuary of the Osirion at Abydos, normally closed to all public traffic.

2. **Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) & Philae Temple Private Hours**
   - **Permit Rate**: ~$2,000 - $4,000 USD depending on VIP hall scope.
   - **Access**: VIP curator-led private walk-throughs of restricted GEM conservation labs and private island access to Philae Temple in Aswan.
