About This Space

Wake Up to the Nile and Ancient Wonders

Imagine looking out from the living room to a breathtaking panorama: the gentle flow of the Nile River right in front of you, the iconic silhouettes of the Giza Pyramids rising on the horizon, and—on clear days—the ancient Saqqara Pyramids resting quietly in the far distance on the left side of your view. Welcome to your luxurious home-away-from-home in the heart of Cairo. Situated on the vibrant Corniche El Nile in Maadi, our 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath apartment blends modern comfort with an unbeatable location.

A Sanctuary of Comfort and Style

While the building itself features a traditional, older Cairo exterior with authentic local character, opening the front door reveals a complete transformation. Remodeled and renovated to high standards, the interior is bright, spacious, and meticulously maintained. Cleanliness is our absolute top priority, ensuring you can unwind in a pristine environment after a day of exploring the city.

Designed for Ease and Luxury

Million-Dollar Views

Enjoy dynamic vistas that shift from golden morning sunrises over the water to dramatic evening sunsets.

Fully Equipped & Connected

Features fast Wi-Fi (57 Mbps), SAT TV, a whole-house water filtration system, universal wall sockets, and USB ports in every corner.

Handicap Accessible

Thoughtfully designed for accessibility, featuring an elevator, step-free interior living, and an accessible master bedroom and bathroom.

Chef-Ready Kitchen & Laundry

Includes full cooking essentials, a refrigerator, oven, stove, microwave, coffee maker, plus an in-unit washer and dryer.

Whether you are here to explore Egypt's ancient history or looking for a peaceful riverside escape, we provide seamless hospitality—including optional airport transfers and customized local tour recommendations. Come experience Cairo from a privileged vantage point very few get to enjoy.

Terms & House Rules

A friendly summary so everyone enjoys a smooth, safe stay.

Check-in & Check-out

  • Check-in is from 3:00 PM. Check-out is by 11:00 AM.
  • Self check-in with a smart lock. Access codes are sent once ID for every occupant has been received and verified.

Guests & Registration

  • Official ID is required for every occupant before check-in, as required by Egyptian regulations.
  • Maximum 6 guests. Unregistered overnight guests are not permitted.
  • Daytime visitors are welcome from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, pre-cleared with us and registered with building security.

Quiet Hours & Conduct

  • Quiet hours run daily from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM.
  • No parties, loud music, subletting or commercial photography.

Smoking & Safety

  • No smoking indoors. Balconies and terraces only. Indoor smoking carries a $200 USD odor-elimination fee.
  • Illegal activity means immediate eviction without refund and a report to the Police or Tourism Police.

Damages

  • Damage, breakage, missing items or stains are charged at full replacement value.
  • Excessive soiling needing specialist cleaning carries a fee of at least $100 USD.

Cancellation (direct bookings)

  • Standard rate: 100% refund 30+ days before check-in, 50% for 14 to 29 days, none inside 14 days.
  • Non-refundable rate: discounted and non-refundable. A 48-hour grace period applies if booked 14+ days ahead.
  • Bookings through Airbnb, Booking.com or Vrbo follow that platform’s policy.
