About This Space

Wake Up to the Nile and Ancient Wonders

Imagine stepping out onto your balcony or looking out from the living room to a breathtaking panorama: the gentle flow of the Nile River right in front of you, the iconic silhouettes of the Giza Pyramids rising on the horizon, and—on clear days—the ancient Saqqara Pyramids resting quietly in the far distance on the left side of your view.

Welcome to your luxurious home-away-from-home in the heart of Cairo. Situated on the vibrant Corniche El Nile in Maadi, our 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath apartment blends modern comfort with an unbeatable location.

Cleanliness is our absolute top priority, ensuring you can unwind in a pristine environment after a day of exploring the city.
