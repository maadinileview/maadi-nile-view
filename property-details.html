Wake Up to the Nile and Ancient Wonders

Imagine stepping out onto your balcony or looking out from the living room to a breathtaking panorama: the gentle flow of the Nile River right in front of you, the iconic silhouettes of the Giza Pyramids rising on the horizon, and—on clear days—the ancient Saqqara Pyramids resting quietly in the far distance on the left side of your view.

Welcome to your luxurious home-away-from-home in the heart of Cairo. Situated on the vibrant Corniche El Nile in Maadi, our 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath apartment blends modern comfort with an unbeatable location.

A Sanctuary of Comfort and Style

While the building itself features a traditional, older Cairo exterior with authentic local character, opening the front door reveals a complete transformation. Remodeled and renovated to high standards, the interior is bright, spacious, and meticulously maintained. Cleanliness is our absolute top priority, ensuring you can unwind in a pristine environment after a day of exploring the city.

Designed for Ease and Luxury

  • Million-Dollar Views: Enjoy dynamic vistas that shift from golden morning sunrises over the water to dramatic evening sunsets.
  • Fully Equipped & Connected: Features fast Wi-Fi (57 Mbps), SAT TV, a whole-house water filtration system, universal wall sockets, and USB ports in every corner.
  • Handicap Accessible: Thoughtfully designed for accessibility, featuring an elevator, step-free interior living, and an accessible master bedroom and bathroom.
  • Chef-Ready Kitchen & Laundry: Includes full cooking essentials, a refrigerator, oven, stove, microwave, coffee maker, plus an in-unit washer and dryer.

Whether you are here to explore Egypt’s ancient history or looking for a peaceful riverside escape, we provide seamless hospitality—including optional airport transfers and customized local tour recommendations. Come experience Cairo from a privileged vantage point very few get to enjoy.

What to Expect / Transparency Section

To ensure your stay is wonderful from the moment you arrive, we believe in complete transparency so expectations match reality:

  • The Building Exterior: This apartment is located in a traditional, older building in the heart of Cairo with a modest, character-filled exterior. Once you cross the threshold, you will enter a modern, luxurious, and fully updated sanctuary.
  • The View: You will enjoy direct views of the Nile River and the Giza Pyramids, with the distant Saqqara Pyramids visible on clear days on the far left of your line of sight.
  • Safety & Security: The building features 24/7 security and exterior property cameras. There are no recording devices or cameras inside the private apartment.
